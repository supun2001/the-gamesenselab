# Outstanding decisions from the supplied privacy draft

These are editorial notes for the site owner, not displayed on the policy page.

- Confirm the legal name of the controller and any required business/contact address. Only the supplied GameSense Lab name and established contact email were used; no identity or address was invented.
- The draft's suggested 12-month / 24-month / 30–90-day retention periods were proposals, not established operations. The page uses retention criteria; choose actual periods and implement them in the service when finalised.
- Decide age eligibility and the handling of children's data. No unapproved 16+ or 18+ age rule was added to the website or signup form.
- Confirm purposes/lawful bases, processor regions, and international-transfer safeguards for the actual deployment.
- Configure and document unsubscribe and account-deletion handling. The website has no automated marketing delivery or self-service deletion yet.
- Review the policy when adding analytics, replay/audio uploads, coaching services, model training, or an external AI provider. These remain future features in the current policy.

Drafting instructions and example placeholders were removed from the customer-facing page. Contact references were consolidated to the user-provided contact@thegamesenselab.com. Current providers include Namecheap Private Email and Google Fonts alongside Supabase, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, and Whop.
